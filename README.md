## Printing

When printing, use **A3** (297 mm×420 mm) and print over the SHORT edge to prevent flipping the template on the second page. The recommended templates are the PDF files `Klausurboegen_Print_Margin/`, because they are more fit for printers that cut of a little margin when printing (generally most printers).
